Source: original work, rendered procedurally for Sonara by scripts/winter/render_backplates.py
(seeded noise, a perspective render of a generated heightmap, a synthetic starfield and
Milky Way). No photographs or third-party images were used. The live aurora is drawn at runtime
by the app's own AGSL shader (sharedUi/src/androidMain/.../theme/AuroraShader.android.kt).
